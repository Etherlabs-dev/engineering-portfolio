## Contra case study

### Evidence-bound strategy and copy workflow

**Challenge:** Generated copy can sound convincing while hiding weak evidence, duplicate strategic angles, stale revisions, or ambiguous approval.

**Solution:** I built a governed workspace where every strategic bet has an explicit contract, evidence links, belief movement, expected learning, and safe success signal.
